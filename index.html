Jump to higher roofs, duck under bars, and collect coins as you run.

Your first rooftop run

  • Press ← or → to change roofs. Jump before moving to a higher roof.
  • Press ↑ or Space to jump over vents and gaps.
  • Hold ↓ to duck under overhead bars. On touch screens, swipe in the direction you want to move.
  • Collect coins and power-ups as you run. Press P to open the shop.
